Contact

If you'd like to set up a free 15-minute intake call or request a training, please use the form below.

This inbox is not constantly monitored and is not intended to be used in an emergency. You can expect a response within 48 business hours. If this is an emergency, go to your nearest emergency room. Please do not put any confidential information in this form, including credit card information or social security number.

If you need to talk to someone immediately, call the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline at 988, the Trans Lifeline at 877-565-8860, or text the Crisis Text Line by texting HOME to 741741.
